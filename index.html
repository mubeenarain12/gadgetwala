FAQ

How do I order?

Choose your phone model, add the product to your cart, enter your name and address, and tap "Send order on WhatsApp". Your order reaches us directly on WhatsApp and we confirm it with you.

How do I pay?

Most orders are sent Cash on Delivery, so you pay when the parcel arrives. Advance payment may be requested for high-value items.

How long does delivery take?

We ship by courier across Pakistan. Delivery time and charges for your city are confirmed on WhatsApp.

What if there is a problem with my product?

Check the parcel as soon as it arrives. If something is wrong, message us on WhatsApp the same day and we will help according to the shop's policy.

Is the stock also available in your shop?

Yes. GadgetWala is the online store of AA Mobile Shop, and the stock is the shop's own.
